# How The Pandemic is Opening New Gateways For the Outsourcing Business.

#### 2 min read by [Yours Truly](https://www.twitter.com/gedionteshome) - September 1, 2020

The scale of impact might vary, but we can argue that COVID-19 has affected everyone on our planet in some way. The chain of events that transpired after that were harsher for some than others. This has forced the world to adopt a new way of doing things. For companies, this meant shifting away from the paradigms of doing business.

Several small businesses are facing risks of bankruptcy and are on the verge of falling to debt. But, well-established businesses only had to deal with the mild change in work-culture. Furthermore, they have been able to withstand the challenge and even grow their profit margins. Due to the new market introduced with the pandemic, most bigger size companies had a surge in their hiring needs. Amazon for example said they would be [adding 100,000 new jobs](https://www.barrons.com/articles/amazon-is-adding-100-000-new-jobs-because-of-coronavirus-demand-heres-a-list-of-other-places-hiring-51584458549) because of the Coronavirus demand.

On the contrary,small business owners and startups are laying off employees to stay afloat. They are looking for outside talents to share their loads at a lower cost. This means they are outsourcing a majority of their processes to cut costs, focus on core business, resolve capacity issues, get better quality, and many other reasons as well,' says [infoq](https://www.infoq.com/articles/outsource-practices-change-2020/). This helps them stay lean and focused on their main revenue-generating service.

[Outsourcing](https://svitla.com/blog/software-outsourcing#:~:text=Software%20Outsourcing%20is%20the%20practice,in%20a%20software%20development%20project.&text=As%20far%20as%20global%20trends,the%20top%20of%20the%20list) is not a new concept per-se and it has been growing with adoption of the world wide web. In simple words, it means obtaining goods or a service by contract from an outside supplier. Countries like India and Bulgaria are well known for their plethora of outsourcing talents. But, in most African countries and some other regions, outsourcing is still a premature industry ripe for the taking.

The shift in hiring strategies and the cheap demand surge might affect the rate of layoffs in some companies. That sucks! But for the African workforce, it is opening a gate of opportunities. The need for trained talents is in-demand. To meet this gap, Both small and well-established companies are looking for remote and external talents. Excited to jump on this ship? Hold your horses, there is a twist to it.

![Hold your horse Gif](https://media.giphy.com/media/lH6KM3wmpG1ck/giphy.gif)

There are several websites like [Fiverr](https://www.fiverr.com/) and [Upwork](https://www.upwork.com/) that can connect you to jobs on-demand. But, big businesses prefer doing business with other businesses. [B2B](https://www.investopedia.com/terms/b/btob.asp) partnership helps both ends of the deal to provide the best service in a trustworthy manner. This helps result in a long-lasting and pleasing transaction.

If you are looking to start a new business but do not have an idea, this model might be worth your time to research. But if you are a student, an unsatisfied employee, or an underpaid freelancer, finding these local and international companies that will help you match with jobs that have high clientele across the world will prove helpful. Although a small list, some African companies that are using this business model to provide solutions are: [Gebeya Inc.](https://www.gebeya.com/), [Andela](https://andela.com/), and [Alama.co](https://alama.co/). I have gotten to know all of them through personal experience. You can read about my experience and learn more about these companies on my next blog.
