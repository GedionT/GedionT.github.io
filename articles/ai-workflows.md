## The Agentic Shift

Software engineering is undergoing its most radical transformation since the invention of the high-level language. We are no longer just writing code; we are orchestrating intelligence.

### Orchestration over Implementation

In an AI-native workflow, my primary role is as an architect and validator. I define the system constraints, and a swarm of specialized agents handles the implementation details.

### RAG as Code Intelligence

Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG) is the backbone of this. By feeding my entire technical stack and project history into a local vector database, I ensure that my AI assistants have perfect context.
